Pakistan as China's Next Manufacturing Base: The China+1 Opportunity, Investment Barriers and Sector Level Competitiveness

ARS-PB-2026-002  |  Reza Khan  |  30 August 2026

A test of the proposition that Pakistan can serve as a viable China+1 manufacturing base. The brief weighs domestic market scale, labour availability and Chinese technology transfer against industrial energy cost, export logistics, regulatory predictability and security, and finds the case holds for import substituting manufacture aimed at Pakistani customers but not for export oriented production. The conclusion is sector specific rather than country level.
