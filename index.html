Latest Research & Insights

ARS POLICY BRIEF  |  ARS-PB-2026-002  |  30 AUGUST 2026

China+1: Can Pakistan Become China's Next Manufacturing Base?

A test of whether Pakistan can serve as a viable China+1 manufacturing base. The brief weighs domestic market scale, labour and Chinese technology transfer against energy cost, export logistics and regulatory predictability, and finds the case holds for import substituting manufacture but not for export oriented production.

POLICY BRIEF  |  ARS-PB-2026-001  |  29 AUG 2026

Beyond the Car Factory: Where Pakistan's EV Opportunity Really Lies

An assessment of Pakistan's electric vehicle transition, examining investment opportunities across batteries, components, charging infrastructure, commercial mobility and two and three wheelers, with particular focus on opportunities for Chinese investors and technology partners.

DATA BRIEF  |  ARS-DB-2026-001  |  28 AUG 2026

The Battery Race: Can Pakistan Catch the EV Wave?

A data driven assessment of Pakistan's National Lithium-Ion Battery Manufacturing Policy 2026–31 and the evolving EV Battery Policy, examining localisation targets, tariff structures and performance-based incentives.

ARS INSIGHT  |  ARS-IN-2026-003  |  27 AUG 2026

The Arbitration Gap: What Pakistan Can Learn from Astana

An analysis of Pakistan's arbitration framework and the lessons from Astana's development as a regional dispute-resolution hub.

ARS INSIGHT  |  ARS-IN-2026-002  |  27 AUG 2026

Plug and Play, or Still Plugging In?

An assessment of Pakistan's CPEC Special Economic Zones, examining their development, institutional framework, investment environment and strategic potential.
