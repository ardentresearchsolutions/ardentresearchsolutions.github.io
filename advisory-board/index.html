Advisory Board Member

Dr. Engr. Sanaullah Aman

Dr. Engr. Sanaullah Aman is a dispute resolution professional, academic, and institutional leader working at the intersection of law, management, and public sector governance.

His foundation includes extensive public sector service as a former officer in the Capital Development Authority (CDA), providing direct experience with administrative systems and complex regulatory dispute environments.

He serves as a Member of the Selection Board of the Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC) and the Association of Consulting Engineers of Pakistan (ACEP). Additionally, he is a Professor of Management and author of the book "Alternative Dispute Resolution in a Globalized World: Law, Practice and Innovation."
