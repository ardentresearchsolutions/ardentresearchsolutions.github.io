Advisory Board Member

Dr. Engr. Sanaullah Aman

Dr. Engr. Sanaullah Aman is a dispute resolution professional, academic, and institutional leader with extensive expertise spanning law, management, engineering, and public sector governance.

He brings extensive public sector experience from his tenure as Member (Engineering) at the Capital Development Authority (CDA), where he gained first hand experience in public administration, institutional decision making, regulatory frameworks, infrastructure development, and complex dispute resolution environments.

Dr. Aman currently serves as a Member of the Selection Board of the Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC) and the Association of Consulting Engineers of Pakistan (ACEP), contributing to professional governance, institutional oversight, and the development of engineering and consulting institutions.
