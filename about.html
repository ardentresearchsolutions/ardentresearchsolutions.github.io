About ARS

Independent research for better decisions.

Ardent Research Solutions (Pvt.) Ltd. is a Pakistan-focused research and policy advisory organisation committed to producing independent, evidence-based analysis.

ARS works across public policy, economic and institutional analysis, governance, development and strategic intelligence.

  • Independent and non-partisan
  • Evidence based and data-driven
  • Policy relevant and actionable
  • Local expertise, global standards
  • Research focused on Pakistan
Founder & Director

Reza Khan

Reza Khan is the founder and director of Ardent Research Solutions (Pvt.) Ltd., an independent research and policy advisory organisation focused on Pakistan. He holds an MPhil in Government and Public Policy from the National Defence University, Islamabad.

His areas of interest include public policy, economic and institutional analysis, governance, development, and evidence-based policymaking. His work focuses on examining Pakistan's policy and institutional challenges through research, analysis, and practical policy solutions.

At ARS, he leads research and advisory initiatives aimed at generating rigorous, independent analysis and translating evidence into practical insights for businesses, institutions, and decision makers.

Director

Dr. Rubina Fareed

Dr. Rubina Fareed is a physician with extensive experience in women’s health, human rights, and community development. She has served in leadership and advisory roles focused on protecting the rights and welfare of women and children, including work with the National Commission on the Rights of Child.

Her work combines medical expertise with a longstanding commitment to women’s empowerment, health awareness, and social welfare. She has received the National Human Rights Award and an Achievement Award from the Ministry of Human Rights, as well as the Fatima Jinnah Gold Medal for her contributions to human rights and community service.

At ARS, she contributes to research and advisory initiatives that bring together institutional experience, evidence-based analysis, and practical perspectives to support better decision-making across businesses, organisations, and public sector institutions.
